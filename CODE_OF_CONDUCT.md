# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to welcoming, safe, and technically rigorous open-source communities. Participate with empathy, assume good faith while examining evidence, and critique ideas without attacking people.

Positive behavior includes giving specific feedback, respecting different backgrounds and communication styles, making uncertainty explicit, crediting prior work, routing contributions to the correct owner, and protecting private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, deliberate misrepresentation, doxxing, credential disclosure, or retaliation against a good-faith reporter.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and public interactions where someone represents a project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Report GitHub-hosted abuse through [GitHub's private content-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For non-confidential moderation, flag the content and request maintainer review without repeating harmful or private material. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement considers context, impact, prior behavior, and safety. Retaliation and knowingly false reports are violations. Good-faith disagreement or an incorrect technical proposal is not misconduct.
